Build the categories in two passes.

Pass 1, draft. Read every presentation (title and abstract). Write a draft list of 10–20 candidate topics in the field's conventional vocabulary.

Pass 2, assign and revise. Assign each presentation to the one to three candidates it substantively belongs to. Then revise the list:
- merge candidates that hold under 3% of presentations into their nearest neighbour, or drop them if they have none;
- split any candidate holding more than 30% of presentations into the distinct areas it is hiding;
- merge candidates whose presentations largely overlap.
Re-assign after revising. Stop when the list is stable, typically 8–15 categories.
